# ADR-0002: fail-closed export and conditional cell model

Accepted 2026-10-12, round 2. Independent review of e6833ae found two fail-open cases: an exhausted 1000-point budget leaves rays 0/30/60/90/120 only, with a 240° closing gap; a second failed radial batch leaves numeric blockers at every ray. Previous acknowledgement plus non-null checks allowed both. The two regression tests first failed against that exact source. Old PASS reports concern their then-tested scope and do not certify NINA safety.

Decision: neither physical nor planning NINA export accepts any supported v1/v2 result. Consent is not a completeness certificate. Runtime schema, seam, radial errors and unknown sources are checked for explanations, but even regular complete ray lattices remain UNCERTIFIED_SPATIAL_COVERAGE. No numeric synthetic closing point is generated. UI buttons stay disabled. Future re-enablement requires reviewed continuous geographic coverage and an independent consumer-import gate.

DETAIL additionally traverses native GeoTIFF pixels, including closed cell footprints intersecting a projected disk. A conservative circle contains each pixel square and the entire specified observer/optical-motion disk. Its angular span touches every overlapping sector, including both sides of north. NoData affects those sectors; incomplete traversal/budget/error invalidates all full-sector bounds. Known-subset upper values remain distinct from full projected-model upper values. These are not point-sample maxima or on-site measurements.

Distances and sectors of this first interval kernel are explicitly EPSG25832 GRID_NORTH under a BILINEAR_SUPPORT_MAX_ENVELOPE reconstruction. Geodesic/true-north certification is NOT_CERTIFIED. Therefore conditional bounds are displayed separately and never silently inserted into the geographic curve or NINA. A native height contributes over a square with half-width one full pixel spacing; this contains its complete bilinear basis support. Interpolated heights are convex combinations and cannot exceed the maximum of contributing native heights. Native PixelIsArea spacing remains 1 m / 0.2 m; support width is 2 m / 0.4 m, not a claimed data resolution change. The new kernel eliminates between-ray omission inside its completed projected raster model, but does not prove a real chimney is present in the capture or a true geographic sector bound.

Positions and horizontal optical movement sum as a continuous disk bound; relative ground height ranges are retrieved across that disk with a one-cell halo. Vertical, ground-height error, and optical downward/upward ranges are propagated into eye minima/maxima. Bounds use the lowest possible eye and highest assumed obstruction height. Missing observer ground data prevents propagation. These are deterministic assumptions, not statistical confidence intervals. Actual measurements remain NOT_MEASURED.

Release gates remain: certified projected-to-geographic enclosure/datum limits, capture/physical bounds, more independent data controls, actual NINA consumer execution and full UI/performance tests. No change to main or deployment authorization.

Refinement: schema astro360-3 explicitly marks nominal sampled maxima and non-safe planning references. The unpublished round-2 v2 prototype used constant footprints; it remains historical evidence and is not silently relabeled. All v1/v2/v3 NINA exports stay denied.
